From the Old Town and the Strip to Montechoro and the surrounding parishes, it's a mix of tourist-era apartment blocks and villas with pools, and it needs an electrician who's comfortable with both .

We cover the full Albufeira area — the Old Town, the Strip, Montechoro, Olhos de Água, Guia and out toward Paderne and Ferreiras — working with holiday let owners, property management companies and expat homeowners who need reliable, English-speaking electrical cover from people who understand how the short-term rental market actually works.

Whether it's a repair to keep a booking on track, a consumer unit upgrade ahead of the season, or a full rewire before a property goes back on the market, we diagnose exactly what's going on and explain it in plain English.

What We Fix

Common Electrical Problems in Albufeira Properties

Albufeira's holiday let market runs at full capacity from May to October, and electrical faults don't wait for a convenient moment. These are the calls we get most often — describe what you're seeing and we'll tell you what it is and get someone to you fast.

Air Conditioning Faults During Peak Season

AC failure in an Albufeira holiday let during July or August is a direct financial problem , not just an inconvenience — guests in a property without working air conditioning during a heatwave won't stay, and the review that follows can affect a listing for months. We attend to AC electrical faults across Albufeira as a priority — units that have tripped the fuse box and won't reset, outdoor compressors that have stopped responding, multi-split systems where one or more indoor heads have gone dead, and units that are running but not cooling properly because the fault is electrical rather than a refrigerant problem. Many Albufeira apartments have AC units added over the years onto circuits that were never sized for the extra load, so we start by identifying the actual cause rather than just resetting the breaker and hoping. We also supply and install new AC systems for owners upgrading a property ahead of the season.

Fuse Box Tripping and Overloaded Circuits

The most common call we get from Albufeira holiday let owners is a fuse box that keeps tripping — usually when the AC kicks in, or when the property is at full occupancy and guests are running every appliance at once. This is almost always a capacity problem rather than a simple fault: a studio apartment wired in the 1970s or 1980s was sized for a couple of lamps and a small heater, and the same flat now running air conditioning, a dishwasher, a washing machine, an electric shower and several device chargers simultaneously is routinely asking more of the installation than it was ever designed to handle. We carry out consumer unit assessments and upgrades for Albufeira properties — replacing undersized distribution boards, separating circuits that are sharing inappropriate loads, and installing RCD protection where it's missing, sized to the property's actual demand rather than its original spec.

Pool and Garden Electrical Faults

Albufeira villas with pools are a significant part of the local holiday-let market, and a pool that isn't working mid-booking is a serious problem for a listing that depends on it. We attend to pool electrical faults across Albufeira — pumps that have tripped and won't reset, underwater lighting where a zone has failed, automated pool heating that's stopped responding to its controller, and garden or irrigation circuits that have gone dead. For rental villas where the pool is a key part of the listing, we treat this as a priority call, and carry out pool pump diagnosis and repair across all major brands, underwater lighting repairs, and new pool electrical installations for properties being upgraded.

Apartment Electrical Faults Between Bookings

Albufeira has a large volume of apartment properties in short-term rental use, from small studios in the Old Town to larger units around Montechoro and Areias de São João. Faults in older blocks often involve a mix of private installation issues and shared building infrastructure — a tripping consumer unit might be the apartment's own wiring, or it might be a fault in the building's communal supply that affects every unit on the same feed. We attend to dead sockets, failed lighting circuits, bathroom and kitchen electrics that have stopped working, and intermittent faults that are difficult to trace without proper diagnostic equipment. For a fault discovered mid-booking, working out clearly whether it's the apartment's own installation or the building's shared systems matters — it determines who's responsible for the fix and how it actually gets resolved.

Full Rewires and Consumer Unit Upgrades for Older Albufeira Properties

A significant share of Albufeira's private housing was built during the 1970s and 1980s tourist-development years, and a good number of these properties have never had their electrical installation properly assessed since. Ceramic fuse boards, aluminium wiring, missing RCD protection and earthing that no longer meets current standards are all common findings in older Albufeira properties. For owners who've bought an older Albufeira villa or apartment and found the electrics inadequate — or who simply want the installation brought up to a safe, modern standard before selling, letting or renovating — we carry out full rewires and consumer unit upgrades , certified where required by Portuguese standards. If you're renovating a property for the holiday let market, doing the electrical upgrade as part of that renovation is usually the most practical approach, and we can quote for it alongside whatever else the renovation involves.

Standing Contact for Albufeira Landlords and Property Managers

Property managers and owners with more than one Albufeira rental are welcome to use us as a standing point of contact rather than starting from scratch with a new call every time something goes wrong. We're happy to be the electrician a management company keeps on hand for whichever property needs attention next, including out-of-hours emergency callouts — attending on instruction, communicating clearly in English, and carrying out work that's properly documented rather than a temporary fix that resurfaces at the next guest changeover.
