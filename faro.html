Faro

Your English-Speaking Electrician in Faro

Faro is different from every other place we cover. As the Algarve's district capital and home to the region's only international airport, it's a genuine working city — a historic centre around the Baixa, a university, and commercial streets with restaurants, offices and retail, alongside a large permanent expat community rather than a seasonal resort crowd. The person calling us in Faro is as likely to be a café owner with a kitchen circuit that's just tripped mid-service as a homeowner in a 1970s apartment block wondering why the lights keep going out.

The property stock reflects that mix. Faro's university and its role as the district capital mean a meaningful share of the city's housing is rented rather than owner-occupied, much of it in multi-storey apartment buildings in the city centre going back to the 1960s and 70s. Move out toward Estoi, Santa Bárbara de Nexe, Conceição or Montenegro, and further still toward São Brás de Alportel , and you're into modern villas and rural quintas on a completely different kind of installation. We work across all of it — city apartment, rental property, commercial unit or countryside villa — everything explained and carried out in English.

Older buildings in the historic centre and the surrounding residential streets tend to share the same issues — wiring and fuse boards that were adequate when the building went up but have never been reassessed since, and consumer units that were never designed for the number of appliances a modern household runs at once. We start by finding out exactly what's there, explain it in plain English, and carry out the repair or rewire properly rather than patching around the underlying problem.

What We Fix

Common Electrical Problems in Faro Properties

From older city-centre apartments and commercial units to villas in the surrounding parishes, these are the calls we get most often in Faro. Describe what you're seeing and we'll tell you what it is and get someone to you.

Older Apartments and Buildings in Faro's City Centre

Faro's Baixa and the residential streets around it have a substantial number of multi-storey apartment buildings and townhouses built between the 1960s and the 1980s , and a lot of them have electrical installations that have never been formally reassessed since. Aluminium wiring in place of copper, ceramic fuse carriers instead of a modern RCD-protected consumer unit, and private installations extended piecemeal over the decades — an extra socket here, a converted room there — are all common finds. Unlike a standalone villa, apartment electrical work in an older Faro building also means working out the boundary between what belongs to the flat and what's part of the building's shared electrical infrastructure. Get that wrong and either the fault doesn't actually get resolved, or someone ends up paying to fix something that was never their responsibility. We assess the installation properly, explain clearly which is which, and carry out the repair or full rewire to bring the property up to a safe, modern standard — certified where Portuguese regulations require it.

Commercial Electrical Work — Restaurants, Offices and Retail

Faro is the one location we cover with a genuine commercial property market alongside its residential one, and commercial electrical work carries different demands to a house or apartment. A restaurant or café kitchen runs heavy, continuous load — extract fans, refrigeration, dishwashers and cooking equipment often drawing on three-phase power , all running at once during service. A fault in that kind of setup isn't just an inconvenience — a tripped circuit or a dead socket mid-service is a direct loss of income for as long as it takes to fix. Office and retail units in some of Faro's older buildings were specified decades ago for a fraction of the electrical demand a modern business places on them — air conditioning, IT equipment and point-of-sale systems all added over time onto a supply that was never sized for it. We carry out fault diagnosis and electrical repairs for Faro businesses, working to current Portuguese commercial electrical standards and certifying the work where regulations require it. If you're fitting out a new commercial unit or taking over an existing premises and aren't sure the electrical supply is adequate, it's worth having it assessed before you commit to anything.

Villas and Rural Properties Around Estoi and Santa Bárbara de Nexe

Outside the city, Faro's surrounding parishes — Estoi, Santa Bárbara de Nexe, Conceição and Montenegro — have a mix of older quintas and newer-build villas, many with pools and garden electrics that need proper outdoor-rated wiring. A tripping fuse box when the air conditioning kicks in usually means the circuit is undersized for the property's current demand — very common where a villa's electrics were installed for a much smaller load than it's carrying now. On rural properties, we also occasionally find the supply itself is struggling, which sometimes means liaising with E-REDES (the network operator that runs the actual distribution grid, not EDP, who just bill for the electricity used) to get the connection itself addressed — we'll tell you plainly if that's what's needed rather than just patching around it.

Faro Landlords and Multi-Property Owners

With a good share of Faro's housing in rental use, landlords with older Faro properties are under increasing pressure to bring installations up to a standard tenants and insurers expect. A consumer unit fitted into a 1970s Faro apartment was sized for a couple of lamps and a small heater — the same flat now running air conditioning, a washing machine, a dishwasher and several device chargers at once will trip repeatedly, and that's a capacity problem rather than a simple fault . We upgrade undersized distribution boards to modern RCD-protected consumer units correctly rated for the property's actual load. For landlords managing more than one Faro rental property, the same assessment and upgrade work can be scoped and scheduled across the portfolio rather than treated as separate one-off jobs.

Just Moved to or Renting in Faro

We regularly help people who've just moved into a Faro property and aren't sure the wiring is safe — or who've taken on a rental with no electricity and don't know where to start. We come out, diagnose exactly what's going on, and explain it in plain English — no jargon, no guesswork, and no promises we can't back up about formal paperwork we don't provide. If the property needs work, we tell you what and why; if it's fine, we tell you that too.
